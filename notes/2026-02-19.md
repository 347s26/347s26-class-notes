        1. [extend (subclass) the default UserModel](https://docs.djangoproject.com/en/6.0/topics/auth/customizing/#extending-django-s-default-user)
        2. [change to a custom user model mid project](https://docs.djangoproject.com/en/6.0/topics/auth/customizing/#changing-to-a-custom-user-model-mid-project)
