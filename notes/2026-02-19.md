### Head's up

1. In the [MDN Django tutorial](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn_web_development/Extensions/Server-side/Django/skeleton_website#hooking_up_the_url_mapper), they pranked you when they made the redirect. your browser will have cached the http301 and then when you work on other django projects that aren't the library, the browser will try to redirect you to /catalog. so clear your browser's history for that url only


3. modifying state of model instances
    1. the example [view function](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn_web_development/Extensions/Server-side/Django/Home_page#creating_the_index_page) was only reading, but it could have been modifying
4. won't auto update view
    1. so like if player on their computer takes turn, for now other player won't know, can manually refresh
5. don't make your own user. there's [one in django already](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn_web_development/Extensions/Server-side/Django/Authentication#creating_users_and_groups)
    1. Likely the best choice is to [have your own model that references the existing user model](https://docs.djangoproject.com/en/6.0/topics/auth/customizing/#referencing-the-user-model)
    2. Even though I'd advise against it, technically you could:
        1. [extend (subclass) the default UserModel](https://docs.djangoproject.com/en/6.0/topics/auth/customizing/#extending-django-s-default-user)
        2. [change to a custom user model mid project](https://docs.djangoproject.com/en/6.0/topics/auth/customizing/#changing-to-a-custom-user-model-mid-project)
